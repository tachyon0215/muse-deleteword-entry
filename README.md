# muse-deleteword-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [The Word You'd Delete From the Dictionary](https://make.fun/bounty/u-puan-the-word-you-d-delete-from-the-dictionary-9e774bc2).

## The entry

> lisp

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: 'moist' is the first pick everyone thinks of and 'synergy' is a familiar corporate-jargon deletion; 'lisp' wins because the word for a speech impediment cannot be pronounced cleanly by its own sufferers, so deleting it is funny at first sight from the word itself, fresh, and exact as one correctly spelled word.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 71b3cd08174a0e3cbfa9c5bf1c8d8f25f4e7eaefe82dfa2be759470ea0307429).
